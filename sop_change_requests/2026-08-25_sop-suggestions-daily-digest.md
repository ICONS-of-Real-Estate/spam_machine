## How to apply

Same caution as the 25 Aug dead-sections request: these are multi-paragraph
blocks, and exact Find & Replace against text extracted from an automated
digest is fragile (curly quotes, em-dashes, line-wrap spaces). Use Find
(Ctrl/Cmd+F) to jump to each **start anchor** and **end anchor** below, then
manually select from the start of the start anchor through the end of the
end anchor and replace/delete. Don't fight Find & Replace if it reports 0
matches — go straight to the manual-selection method.

## Change 3 — give a concrete price anchor

**Start anchor**: wherever pricing/cost is currently addressed in the SOP
(likely near "nice dinner and a bottle of wine" if that phrase is in
there, or the general pricing-objection guidance)

**Replace with** (append or replace):

```
When asked generally about cost, don't lead with a number: "It really depends on the level of production, amount of content repurposing, and overall strategy you'd want around the show. ## What NOT to copy from the digest
```

The routing detail baked into several examples ("if a phone number was
given, Sean calls; otherwise, book Joana's own Zoom link") is **already
handled in code** (`CONFIG.SEAN_QUALIFICATION_CALL_URL`, `needsTeammateRouting`
in `Code.gs`, wired 25 Aug). Don't add a competing version of that logic
as free-form SOP text — it risks drifting out of sync with what the code
actually decides. If the routing rule itself needs to change, that's a
code change, not an SOP edit.
